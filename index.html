01 — The vision

One system, instead of five that barely talk.

The future Praxentis Health platform brings the core of running an allied-health practice into a single, coherent place.

Bookings

Scheduling that understands how a clinic actually fills a day — practitioners, rooms, recalls and gaps in one view.

Records

Clinical records designed for allied health from the first field — not a medical template bent into shape.

Workflows

Intake, safety screening, outcomes and follow-up as connected steps, each one informed by the last.

Clinic management

The operational layer — team, communication, reporting — so owners run the practice from one screen, not five tabs.

02 — Why it matters

Clinic software hasn't earned its keep.

Most of what clinics run on today was designed for someone else's practice, another decade, or another country's rules. Praxentis builds inside real clinics — and the same problems surface again and again.

The fix isn't another bolt-on. It's a system designed, end to end, for how allied health actually works.

  • 01

    Fragmented

    Bookings in one tool, records in another, reminders in a third — and the receptionist is the integration layer.

  • 02

    Rigid

    Workflows a clinic can't change without a support ticket, built for a generic practice rather than a real one.

  • 03

    Outdated

    Interfaces from another era, hosted offshore, with privacy posture treated as a footnote instead of a feature.

03 — Design principles

What Praxentis Health will inherit.

The concept doesn't start from zero. It inherits the operating discipline the studio already applies to everything it ships.

PRINCIPLE 01

Piloted in real clinics

Nothing ships to other practices that hasn't earned its place inside an actual one. Every Praxentis product is exercised in real clinics — one in Adelaide, one in Sydney — before release. Health will be no different.

PRINCIPLE 02

Australian data residency

Patient data never leaves the country. Infrastructure in Australia, alignment with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles — a brand pillar, not a compliance afterthought.

PRINCIPLE 03

Calm over clever

Stable, premium, operational software. Fewer moving parts, clearer decisions — a system that feels like relief on a busy day, never another thing to manage.
